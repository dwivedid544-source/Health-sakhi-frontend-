4.  Women&apos;s Health — Specific Scope Limitations



HealthSakhi addresses a wide range of women&apos;s health topics. The following specific limitations apply to these content areas:

Menstrual and Reproductive Health


Cycle tracking information is educational only and does not constitute contraceptive advice


HealthSakhi does not provide contraception guidance, fertility treatment plans, or ovulation-based family planning as clinical services


Content on periods, PCOS, endometriosis, and related conditions is informational — diagnosis and treatment must be provided by a gynaecologist



Hormonal Health


Content on hormonal imbalance, thyroid, insulin resistance, and related topics is educational


Hormonal testing, interpretation of laboratory results, and treatment decisions require qualified medical supervision


Do not start, stop, or modify hormone therapy, contraceptive medication, or thyroid medication based on content in this app



Pregnancy and Postpartum


No content in this app should be used to manage a pregnancy independently


Antenatal care, postnatal care, and breastfeeding guidance must be provided by qualified midwives, obstetricians, or paediatricians


Content on postpartum depression or mood changes requires professional mental health support — not self-management



Menopause and Perimenopause


Content on menopause is educational and aimed at awareness and understanding


Hormone replacement therapy and other menopausal treatments require individual assessment by a qualified specialist



Intimate and Sexual Health


Content on intimate health is educational, culturally sensitive, and designed for awareness


Diagnosis and treatment of intimate health conditions including infections, STIs, and pain disorders require clinical care


The app does not provide sexual health screening, testing, or treatment services




5.  Mental and Emotional Health Disclaimer



HealthSakhi includes content on emotional wellbeing, stress, anxiety, body image, eating behavior, grief, relationship dynamics, trauma awareness, and personal growth. This content is provided for reflection, self-awareness, and general education only.

Limitations of Emotional Health Content


Emotional and psychological content is not therapy, counselling, or psychiatric treatment


Content exploring difficult emotional experiences such as grief, shame, trauma, or loss is for reflective purposes only


Self-reflection exercises and journaling prompts are wellness tools, not clinical interventions


Information about emotional eating, disordered eating patterns, or body image is educational — clinical eating disorders require specialist support



When to Seek Immediate Professional Mental Health Support


Persistent depression, hopelessness, or inability to function in daily life


Active thoughts of self-harm, self-injury, or suicide


Severe anxiety, panic attacks, or dissociation


Trauma responses, flashbacks, or post-traumatic stress


Symptoms of psychosis including hallucinations or delusions


Severe disordered eating including restriction, purging, or binge eating


Emotional abuse, controlling relationships, or domestic violence



HealthSakhi is not a crisis platform. It does not provide 24-hour emergency mental health support. If you are in crisis, please contact a mental health professional or emergency service immediately.
