# Business Requirements

health sakhi is a comprehensive digital wellness platform designed specifically for women's holistic well-being. It leverages technology to guide women through physical, emotional, and financial health journeys.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Sisterly & Trustworthy:** Friendly, empathetic interface designed to build immediate trust.
- **Empowerment-Focused:** Combines physical health tracker modules with career, financial, and spiritual wisdom.
- **Accessible Support:** Provides direct, private advisor booking options alongside 24/7 empathetic AI support.

## Feature Stack ("Wellness Stack")
1. **Women's Health Hub:** PCOD, hormone care, and thyroid balance support.
2. **Emotional Healing:** Guided meditation, mindfulness, and breathing exercises.
3. **AI Sakhi Chat:** An empathetic, private 24/7 conversational companion.
4. **Wellness Books Library:** High-quality digital publications covering parenting, relationships, and self-care.
5. **Video Courses:** Educational modules on life stages, diet, and nutrition.
6. **Advisor Sessions:** Direct 1-on-1 scheduling with certified medical/wellness practitioners.
7. **Wealth & Finance Sakhi:** Budget tracking, household financial management, and goal planners.
8. **Daily To-Do & Habits:** Daily productivity and wellness routine manager.
9. **Affirmations:** Positive thinking and self-love card generator.
10. **Spiritual Sakhi:** Solutions and inner peace tips based on Bhagavad Gita wisdom.

## Pricing & Membership Structure

### 1. Sakhi Free
- Basic content access (10 videos, 5 books).
- Limited AI chat (5 chats/day cap).
- 1-on-1 consultation scheduling is blocked (upgrade prompt displays).

### 2. Sakhi Premium Pro
- Unlimited educational content and book library access.
- Unlimited AI Sakhi chats.
- Direct booking for advisor sessions unlocked.
- Premium status badge on profile and receipts download enabled.

### 3. Sakhi Elite
- Priority advisor matching.
- Personal diet & custom nutrition logs.
- Up to 4 free advisor sessions per month.
